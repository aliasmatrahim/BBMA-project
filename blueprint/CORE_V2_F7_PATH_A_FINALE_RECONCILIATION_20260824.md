Around 00:02:23–00:04:15 the speaker emphasizes that historically the focus was first on correct entry, and says TP is secondary to correct entry. This section does not define a universal Re-Entry TP level.

### 2.2 TP Wajib is explicitly attached to the Extreme stage

Around 00:06:46–00:07:10 the speaker maps the BBMA process:

- Extreme occurs first;
- after Extreme there must be a `TP Wajib`;
- the Extreme entry has TP Wajib;
- then the process moves into Market Hilang Volume (MHV).

This strongly confirms that `TP Wajib` is a specific Extreme-stage obligation in this teaching sequence, not evidence that every Re-Entry uses the same static destination.

Around 00:07:47–00:08:20 the speaker describes the subsequent confirmation / second entry stage and then Momentum beginning the journey.

This is consistent with:

`... direction / confirmation -> Re-Entry -> Momentum ...`

but does not itself provide a deterministic `no Momentum after Re-Entry = TP` Boolean rule.
