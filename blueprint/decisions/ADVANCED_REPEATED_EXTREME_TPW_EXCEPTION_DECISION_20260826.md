## Final rule

### Normal default
A normal standalone Extreme retains normal TP-Wajib.

### Bounded advanced exception
A later repeated same-direction LTF Extreme may be interpreted as **nested continuation/completion without requiring a new local TP-Wajib/MHV cycle** only when all of the following are true:

1. there are multiple source-valid same-direction LTF Extreme events in one structural sequence;
2. no source-valid opposite LTF Extreme intervenes;
3. an immediate higher timeframe independently owns a source-valid, mature MHV/MLV or Re-Entry compatible with that sequence;
4. the higher-timeframe owner is established by its own canonical detector/lifecycle — it is not inferred as confirmed merely from the LTF Extreme count;
5. the current LTF Extreme still satisfies its own canonical validity and maturity requirements; HTF context cannot remotely mature a raw LTF Extreme;
6. no canonical Momentum/Extreme invalidation or higher-owner conflict supersedes the sequence.

Under those conditions, the ordinary expectation `every LTF Extreme must create a fresh local TPW -> local MHV cycle before nested continuation can be read` is not required.

## What this exception does NOT mean

- one Extreme may skip TP-Wajib merely because it is called advanced;
- `Extreme Magic` automatically skips TP-Wajib;
- two H1 Extremes automatically confirm H4 MHV;
- a raw LTF Extreme becomes immediate execution authority;
- a lower-timeframe exception overrides the actual HTF owner;
- the exception changes REM/RRE/REE signatures;
- the exception creates an extra broker order or direct standalone-Extreme path in the current EA;
- every higher-timeframe Extreme may skip TP-Wajib.
